SERVICES

Technology, digital growth and media services.

BLUE EARTHE PTE. LTD. provides practical consulting and execution services for businesses and digital publishers. Each engagement is scoped separately based on the client’s objectives, systems and operating requirements.

01

Technology & IT Consulting

Technical consulting for digital products, websites, data workflows, integrations and operational automation. Work may include architecture review, implementation planning, workflow design and technical coordination.

02

Performance Marketing

Planning and optimization of measurable digital acquisition programs. We use campaign and conversion data to inform decisions; we do not promise guaranteed ROI or specific commercial outcomes.

03

Media Buying

Media planning and buying across relevant digital channels and formats, with spend, targeting, measurement and optimization aligned to an agreed campaign scope.

04

Paid Search & Paid Social

Account and campaign setup, audience or keyword planning, creative testing, measurement and ongoing optimization for paid search and social campaigns.

05

User / Customer Acquisition

Acquisition planning for businesses, websites, apps and digital products, supported by funnel analysis and campaign measurement.

06

Content Marketing

Content planning and campaign-oriented creative support, including landing-page and performance-content work where included in the engagement.

07

Marketing Analytics

Reporting structures, measurement frameworks and analysis that help teams understand media, acquisition and monetization performance.

08

Campaign Optimization

Structured analysis and testing across audiences, creative, media and conversion paths, using observed performance to guide changes.

09

Publisher Monetization

Monetization strategy and operations for digital publishers, including pricing experimentation, yield analysis, reporting, traffic-quality controls and ad operations support.
